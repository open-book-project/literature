    About this book¶

    Tales of witches and wizards, tsars' sons and clever peasants, talking beasts and magic gifts, from the great Russian collection.

    From Standard Ebooks · CC0 1.0

    Alexander Afanasyev (1826–1871), Narodnye russkie skazki (Russian), 1855–1863, selected and translated by Leonard A. Magnus (1879–1924), 1915. This book is not stored in this library: each chapter is read from the public repository of its Standard Ebooks edition when it is opened, shown in this library's format, and ends with its own attribution. Standard Ebooks edition, CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication); transcription originally from Project Gutenberg. Modified: converted to this format.
