    About this book¶

    Six Latin plays of martyrs, converts and steadfast women by a tenth-century Saxon canoness, the first known woman dramatist of Europe.

    From Standard Ebooks · CC0 1.0

    Roswitha (Hrotsvitha) of Gandersheim (about 935–about 973), plays (Latin), translated by Christopher St. John (Christabel Marshall, 1871–1960), 1923, with an introduction by Cardinal Gasquet (1846–1929). This book is not stored in this library: each chapter is read from the public repository of its Standard Ebooks edition when it is opened, shown in this library's format, and ends with its own attribution. Standard Ebooks edition, CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication); transcription originally from the Internet Archive. Modified: converted to this format.
