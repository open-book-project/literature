    description: How to put The Open Book Project: Literature on an Android phone or tablet, an iPhone or iPad, or a computer, as an app that opens full screen and works without a connection.¶

    Get the app¶

    The Open Book Project: Literature is an app that lives on this website. Put it on your device once and it opens full screen from its own icon, without the browser around it, and the books you have kept on the device can be read without a connection. It is the same library, and there is nothing to sign in to.

    Android phone or tablet

    This device
    1. Open this site in Chrome.
    2. Open the menu (the three dots) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
    3. Tap Install. The app is now on your home screen and opens full screen.

    iPhone and iPad

    This device
    1. Open this site in Safari. Other browsers on an iPhone or iPad cannot add it to the home screen.
    2. Tap the Share button (at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone, at the top on an iPad).
    3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    The Open Book Project: Literature now opens full screen from its own icon, with no address bar, and keeps working without a connection for the books you have kept on the device.

    Windows, macOS, ChromeOS and Linux

    This device
    1. Open this site in Chrome or Edge.
    2. Click the Install icon at the right of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow). Or open the browser menu and choose Install (in Edge: Apps, then Install this site as an app).
    3. Confirm with Install. The app opens in a window of its own and can be pinned to the taskbar or the dock.

    Safari on a Mac (macOS Sonoma or later): File, then Add to Dock.

    Reading without a connection

    Whichever way you installed it, open a book and choose Keep for offline reading (on its cover or its first page). The whole book is then stored on your device. When a new edition of the site is published, a small notice offers to reload, and kept books are brought up to date the next time you are online.

    Your place, bookmarks and notes stay on the device. More about reading here is on the About page.
